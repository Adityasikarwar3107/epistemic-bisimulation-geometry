For random navigation, priority changes have no effect by construction. On the corridor, the paired change in mean steps (propagated minus source-only) is $0.000$ with a 95\% seed-bootstrap interval $[0.000,0.000]$. On the branching-return task, the paired change in mean steps (propagated minus source-only) is $0.125$ with a 95\% seed-bootstrap interval $[0.000,0.375]$. These tasks do not demonstrate a systematic acquisition advantage from propagated priorities.
